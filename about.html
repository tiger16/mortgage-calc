About EasyLoanCalculator

EasyLoanCalculator is a free tool for estimating monthly payments on mortgages, auto loans, personal loans, and student loans. Enter your loan amount, rate, and term to get an instant breakdown — including total interest, a year-by-year amortization schedule, and how much you could save by paying extra toward principal.

Who it's for

Anyone shopping for a loan, comparing offers from different lenders, or trying to understand how extra payments affect their payoff timeline. It's built for quick "what-if" scenarios before you talk to a lender — not as a replacement for one.

How the math works

Payments are calculated using the standard fixed-rate amortization formula used throughout the lending industry, based on principal, monthly interest rate, and number of payments. The amortization schedule then simulates each monthly payment to show exactly how the principal/interest split shifts over the life of the loan.

What it isn't

This is an educational estimation tool, not a lender, broker, or source of financial advice. See our Disclaimer for full details, and always confirm real terms with a licensed lender before making a borrowing decision.
