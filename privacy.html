Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how EasyLoanCalculator ("we," "the site") handles information when you use this loan and mortgage calculator.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The calculator runs entirely in your browser. The loan amount, interest rate, term, and any other figures you enter are never transmitted to a server or stored by us — they exist only in your browser's memory while the page is open, and disappear when you close or refresh the tab.

2. No Financial Account Linking

This site does not connect to bank accounts, credit bureaus, or lenders, and does not ask for sensitive identifiers such as your Social Security number, full name, or account numbers.

3. Cookies and Advertising

This site may display advertisements served by third-party ad networks, including Google AdSense. These networks may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites.

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising via Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also review the Digital Advertising Alliance's opt-out tool at optout.aboutads.info.

4. Analytics

We may use aggregate, privacy-respecting analytics to understand overall traffic patterns (e.g. how many people use the tool). This data is not tied to your entered loan figures and is not used to identify individual visitors.

5. Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this policy periodically. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed via the contact details on our About page.
